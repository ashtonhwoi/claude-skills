# Per-Company Coverage Areas

What each dedicated analyst subagent should track. Add companies as needed using the same pattern.

## Meta
Facebook, Instagram, Threads, WhatsApp, Messenger, Meta AI, Llama models, Quest VR, Ray-Ban Meta, Orion AR, advertising platform, creator tools, developer APIs.
Focus: new AI products, ad technology, algorithm updates, creator economy, wearables, AR/VR, smart glasses, AI assistants.
Analyze reactions of creators, advertisers, users, Wall Street. Competes vs Apple, Google, TikTok, OpenAI.

## Apple
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, AirPods, Apple Intelligence, Siri, iOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS, developer announcements, hardware, software updates.
Focus: major iOS releases, Apple Intelligence, chip developments, privacy features, design language, developer ecosystem.
Always explain why Apple introduced a feature, who benefits, user + developer reaction, competitor response, and Wall Street view.

## NVIDIA
New GPUs, AI chips, CUDA, DGX, Blackwell, Rubin, RTX, enterprise AI, data centers, robotics, autonomous driving, gaming, cloud partnerships.
Focus: AI infrastructure, cloud providers, GPU demand, supply chain, semiconductor competition.
Explain why NVIDIA built it, which industries benefit, customer demand, stock reaction, and how AMD/Intel/custom AI chips (TPU, Trainium) affect NVIDIA.

## Google / Alphabet
Android, Gemini, Search, Chrome, Google Cloud, Pixel, Waymo, DeepMind, Workspace, YouTube, developer APIs, AI Studio, Vertex AI, TPUs.
Focus: Gemini updates, Android releases, AI products, Search changes, Cloud AI, developer tools.
Explain how Google competes vs OpenAI, Apple, Microsoft. Cover both consumer reaction and enterprise adoption.

## OpenAI
ChatGPT, GPT models, reasoning models, image generation, voice, API, Codex, Operator/agents, Deep Research, enterprise, education, developer platform, Sora.
Focus: new model releases, pricing, capabilities, benchmarks, enterprise adoption, safety, developer tools, funding/corporate structure.
Explain why the update matters, which businesses benefit, competitor response (Google, Anthropic, Meta, xAI), and the future shape of the AI industry.
Note: private company — cover valuation/funding instead of stock price.

## SpaceX
Starship, Falcon, Dragon, Starlink, launches, NASA missions, government + military contracts, satellite deployments, Mars program.
Focus: successful launches, failures/anomalies, engineering milestones, reusable rockets, Starlink expansion (incl. Direct-to-Cell), space economy.
Explain what happened, why it matters, engineering + business + government significance, and impact on Blue Origin, Rocket Lab, ULA.
Note: cover valuation/funding; if public, cover stock.

## Adding other companies
For Microsoft, Amazon, Tesla, Anthropic, xAI, etc.: list the core products/domains, the key focus areas, whose reactions to analyze, and the main competitors — then reuse the same report structure.
